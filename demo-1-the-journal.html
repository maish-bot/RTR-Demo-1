Who we are

Read the Room began in 2025 as a handful of friends meeting up to talk about the books they couldn’t stop thinking about. It’s grown by word of mouth into a proper community, with new faces every month, one book at a time.

No pressure to have finished it, no judgment if you didn’t. Just good conversation, good company, and better book recommendations than any algorithm.

Every book so far

August 2026The Seven Husbands of Evelyn HugoTaylor Jenkins Reid+

Our biggest turnout yet. Everyone had a theory about Evelyn by the interval, and the debate carried on well past closing time.

“I did not expect to cry into my wine over a fictional Hollywood starlet, but here we are.” Priya
June 2026Lessons in ChemistryBonnie Garmus+

A summer rooftop meet-up with iced tea and strong opinions about Elizabeth Zott. Several members left wanting to take up rowing.

“Six-Thirty might be the best character in anything I’ve read this year.” Maya
April 2026Tomorrow, and Tomorrow, and TomorrowGabrielle Zevin+

A quieter, more reflective night. This one split the room, which made for one of our best discussions yet.

“Genuinely changed how I think about creative partnership.” Aisha
February 2026CirceMadeline Miller+

Our first mythology pick, complete with a homemade Greek-inspired spread from one of our members. We may make this a tradition.

“Made me want to read every myth I half-remember from school again.” Priya
